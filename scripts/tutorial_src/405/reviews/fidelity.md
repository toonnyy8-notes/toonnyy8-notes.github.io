# 405 design.md fidelity review (Goldfish)

Setup: check.py ran cleanly; every printed number matches section 5 (C1, C2, C3, C4, Tab 1/4/5). Spot-checked against paper.txt: Tab 1-10, Eq 4-8/17, Alg 2-4, Sec 4-7, A.1-A.7. All transcribed paper numbers are correct (including R1/R2 inconsistencies). Findings below are about claims, not transcription.

1. **P1** LO4 / step 2 / R5: "分布距離（Tab. 1）在網路完美時為 0", "Tab. 1 才是只反映誤差的量", "Tab. 1 協定沒有這個問題". Paper Sec. 4: longer chains "more easily approximated with a Gaussian", i.e. the reverse step has a Gaussian/fixed-variance error independent of the network. The designer's own check.py gives FD(delta=0) = 4.0e-6 (nonzero, because fixed beta-tilde is not the true reverse variance), and the real FID has a 50k-sample floor (ADM-IP 0.972 vs ADM 0.983 at 100 steps, a 1.1% "gap"). Fix: "接近 0 (只剩固定變異數與有限樣本誤差)"; do not call Tab. 1 pure network error; do not present the 1.1% as an effect.
2. **P1** step 6 / LO10 / C3: "DDPM-IP 等於...隨 t 變化的高斯模糊". The paper says nothing of blur (Sec. 5.2: "smooth its prediction function", similar to VRM). It is additive noise on the data, with t-dependent std; "模糊" suggests image blur. Fix: say "加噪 (資料分布與高斯卷積)", drop "模糊/鄰域大" wording or tag it 〔本頁比喻〕.
3. **P1** C3 observation 1: "高噪聲步的網路被要求對很大的鄰域給出相同目標", blur std 0.636 at t=900. Misleading: in x_t space the extra noise is gamma*sqrt(1-abar) <= 0.1 on an input already of std ~1, i.e. noise power x(1+gamma^2) at every t (Eq. 7). The x0-space divergence is just division by tiny sqrt(abar). Not in the paper. Fix: lead with the constant relative factor (1+gamma^2); present x0-space blur only as a unit conversion (R3).
4. **P1** step 6 slope toy / C3 / LO10: "IP 確實讓預測函數更平滑". The shrinkage c_IP/c is just MMSE attenuation for a noisier input. Computed here: DDPM-y (target eps') has slope ratio 1.004 / 0.997 / 0.995 at t=100/500/900 (gamma=0.1) - also "smoothed" or even steeper, yet Tab. 7 says DDPM-y is worse. The toy cannot separate IP from DDPM-y, and the effect is <=1%. Add this caveat; do not let the toy support "平滑 explains IP".
5. **P1** R4: "以 check.py 試算確認 ... 零平均高斯誤差被取樣鏈衰減；IP 只會讓結果變差". check.py has no such computation (grep: none). Stated as verified but is not. Add the script or soften to "未驗證".
6. **P2** step 2 〔本頁推導〕: "確定性鏈只能回到與 x_t 相容的某個 x0". The mean-only chain does not return a sample of q(x0|x_t); it collapses toward a mode/mean (the toy gives L1 = E|x0| = 0.399 at t=1000, i.e. output 0). The paper's wording (A.1): the deterministic process "target[s] the mode". Fix: "回到 x_t 的某個確定性函數，通常是模態/平均，不是原圖".
7. **P2** LO4 reference answer also gives the answer as "確定性鏈無法逐樣本還原"; fine, but it should be tagged that the paper attributes Tab. 6 growth wholly to error (R5 does this; the LO answer omits it).
8. **P2** C4 table 5.: column "最佳 T'" lists CelebA IP = 1000 (1.31) and FFHQ IP = 1000. Paper Sec. 6.2: CelebA IP gets 1.27 at T'=900. Header must read "Tab. 3 格點內最佳"; as written it contradicts the paper's own 1.27.
9. **P2** hero 1.1 / fact 3: "CelebA 64 用 900 步達到 FID 1.27 ... CelebA 節省 37.5% 訓練時間" in one sentence implies one model. Tab. 4: 37.5% is ADM-IP 300K iters, 200 steps, FID 1.53; 1.27 is a different checkpoint/setting (training length not stated). Abstract juxtaposes them too; separate them.
10. **P2** hero: "達到 ADM 1,000 步的 FID 只需 60–200 步" and "FID 全面下降". Paper's evidence is Tab. 4 (one FID per dataset, ImageNet 3.50 vs 3.53 and CIFAR 2.93 vs 2.99, margins inside the R1 inconsistency). Say "與...相當或略優".
11. **P2** step 1 / common error 4: "Alg. 2 的 σ_t z 應使用標準差 sqrt(β̃)" is the designer's reading (paper writes sigma_t z and N(., sigma_t I) with same sigma_t). Untagged; mark 〔本頁判讀〕 (R7 half-says it).
12. **P2** step 6: grid-search rationale omits paper's phrase "covering the last half of the sampling trajectory"; and gamma=0.1 as chosen vs nu in [0,0.2]: paper never states the grid points besides Fig. 4 (0.05, 0.1, 0.2). "網格搜尋得 0.1" is fine, but LO9 should not imply a finer grid.
13. **P2** common error 5: "respacing（依選出的時間點重算 β）" is Nichol & Dhariwal's method; paper only cites it. Tag source.
14. **P2** C1 title concept "曝光偏差玩具": delta-bias model is a deterministic proportional bias, not the paper's zero-mean Gaussian error (disclosed). Add that a constant-ratio bias cannot be what Sec. 5.1 simulates, so C1 does not support the mechanism of IP.
15. **P2** step 9: Tab. 9 CelebA/CIFAR "訓練迭代 540K" vs Tab. 4 ADM 480K/500K; not flagged in R1. List as paper inconsistency.
